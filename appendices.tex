\section{Energy density data}\label{app:cert}
Tables~\ref{tab:energy-density} and~\ref{tab:energy-tail} list the exact integers of Proposition~\ref{prop:energy}.
The cell indexed by $i$ is $[1/2+i/128,1/2+(i+1)/128)$, and its density is $n_{i,q}/2^{40}$.
Table~\ref{tab:energy-density} gives $0\le i\le145$. For $146\le i\le255$, $n_{i,67}=n_{i,70}=0$ except at the indices of Table~\ref{tab:energy-tail}.
Thin spaces group the digits in threes.
